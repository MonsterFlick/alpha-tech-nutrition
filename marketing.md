# Alpha Tech Nutrition - Gym Supplements Landing Page

## Title
**Alpha Tech Nutrition - High-Performance Gym & Sports Supplements Landing Page**

## Description
A bold, premium gym supplement landing page designed for athletes and fitness enthusiasts. Features a striking dark theme with electric lime accents, ultra-smooth Lenis scrolling, and sophisticated Framer Motion micro-animations throughout.

### Key Features
- **Lenis Smooth Scrolling** - Buttery smooth scroll experience with customized lerp and duration settings
- **Framer Motion Animations** - Spring-based parallax, staggered reveals, 3D tilt cards, and magnetic hover effects
- **Click Spark Effect** - Interactive spark burst animation on every click for enhanced engagement
- **Glassmorphism Navigation** - Sticky header with blur backdrop that transforms on scroll
- **Interactive Product Carousel** - Smooth product showcase for Prime Whey, Anabolic Lean Muscle Builder, and Alpha Super Mass Gainer
- **3D Bento Grid** - Mouse-tracking tilt cards with animated borders and shine effects highlighting formula benefits
- **Mobile-First Responsive** - Fully optimized for all screen sizes

### Featured Products
1. **Prime Whey** - 100% Ultra-Filtered Whey Protein with 25g protein and 5.5g BCAAs
2. **Anabolic Lean Muscle Builder** - Strength & Hypertrophy Matrix for shredded, dense muscle gains
3. **Alpha Super Mass Gainer** - High-Calorie Muscle Mass Matrix with 50g protein and clean carbs

### Design Highlights
- Royal Midnight Navy (#0B0E23 / #161B3D) and Cobalt Blue (#3B82F6) matched to supplement packaging
